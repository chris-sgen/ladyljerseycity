What Is 420?

The story behind 420 is more grounded than the myths suggest. In 1971, five friends at San Rafael High School in California started using "420" as private code. They called themselves the Waldos, and their routine was simple: meet at 4:20 PM near a campus statue, then set out to find a rumored, abandoned cannabis grow near Point Reyes. They never found the grow, but the phrase stuck.

From "420 Louis" to just "420," the term spread quickly once the Waldos connected with the Grateful Dead's inner circle. It moved through the Deadhead community, crossed into broader cannabis culture, and eventually turned the date itself, April 20, into an unofficial global holiday. Today, 4/20 is a day for community gatherings, cannabis celebrations, and for places like Lady L, a chance to honor the culture and reward our customers.

The Significance of 420 in Cannabis Culture

420 is more than a date on the calendar. For decades, it served as a rallying point for communities that fought hard to change the narrative around cannabis. The conversations that happened on April 20, across college campuses, at music festivals, and in living rooms, helped push legalization onto the national agenda. New Jersey's adult-use market is a direct product of that advocacy.

Today, 420 is a celebration of how far the culture has come. It's a moment to gather, to educate, and to appreciate the community built around cannabis. What are the legal purchase limits in New Jersey?

New Jersey allows dispensaries to sell up to the equivalent of 28.35 grams (1 ounce) of usable cannabis per transaction. That limit can be any combination of the following.

  • Dried flower/bud: 28.35 grams (1 ounce)
  • Concentrates/resin/oils: 4 grams (solid or liquid/vaporized oil)
  • Ingestible items (gummies/edibles): 1,000 mg of THC (roughly ten 100mg packages)
  • Maximum possession: up to 6 ounces of cannabis and cannabis products

You can mix and match across categories. The 1-ounce limit is generally treated as a daily limit across all dispensaries.

What taxes apply to adult-use cannabis in New Jersey?

Adult-use cannabis in New Jersey is subject to a 6.625% state sales tax, a 2% municipal transfer or user tax where applicable, and a $2.50 per ounce Social Equity Excise Fee (SEEF). All taxes are applied at checkout. No surprises.
