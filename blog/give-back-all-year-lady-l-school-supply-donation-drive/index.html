Year-Round School Supply Donations in Jersey City | Lady L

10/10/2026

School Supplies Run Out. Our Support Doesn’t. 💜

Lady L’s Year-Round School Supply Donation Drive in Jersey City

Because supporting our community shouldn’t be seasonal.

Every September, communities come together to help students start the school year with fresh notebooks, sharpened pencils, new backpacks, and everything they need to succeed.

But what happens when September ends?

The need for school supplies doesn’t disappear when the back-to-school season is over. Notebooks fill up, pencils get lost, backpacks wear out, and teachers often find themselves purchasing classroom essentials out of their own pockets.

At Lady L Jersey City, we believe that supporting local students, educators, and families should be a year-round commitment.

That’s why our Back-to-School Donation Drive continues all 365 days of the year.

Why Year-Round School Supply Donations Matter

Education is one of the foundations of a strong, thriving community. Yet for many families, keeping up with the ongoing cost of school supplies can be challenging.

A single school year requires more than one shopping trip. Students continuously need fresh materials, and teachers frequently replenish classroom essentials to ensure everyone has the tools to participate and learn.

By keeping our donation initiative active throughout the year, we hope to encourage a simple but important shift in thinking:

Giving back isn’t something we do once a year. It’s something we build into everyday life.

How You Can Help Jersey City Students and Teachers

Supporting our local classrooms doesn’t require a major financial commitment. Small, thoughtful contributions can add up to meaningful community impact.

Lady L welcomes donations of new, unused school supplies, including:

  • 📓 Notebooks, composition books, and loose-leaf paper
  • ✏️ Pencils, pens, erasers, and sharpeners
  • 🖍️ Crayons, markers, and colored pencils
  • 📁 Folders, binders, and pencil cases
  • 🎒 Backpacks and lunch bags
  • 📏 Rulers, glue sticks, and other classroom essentials

Whether you’re clearing out unused supplies at home, shopping for your own family, or simply looking for an easy way to give back, every contribution is appreciated.

Building a Stronger West Side, Together

Lady L is proud to be part of Jersey City’s West Side community.

As a locally owned small business, we believe our responsibility extends beyond our storefront. We want to contribute to a neighborhood where families feel supported, educators feel appreciated, and children have opportunities to thrive.

Community involvement doesn’t always require a large event or elaborate initiative. Sometimes it starts with a notebook, a box of pencils, or a neighbor choosing to help another neighbor.

When local businesses and residents work together, even the smallest acts of generosity can create lasting connections.

Make Giving Back Part of Your Routine

The next time you’re shopping for school supplies, consider picking up one extra item for a local student.

Have unused supplies sitting in a drawer? Give them a purpose.

Want to teach your children about community responsibility? Invite them to help choose supplies to donate.

These everyday actions help create a culture of generosity that reaches beyond the classroom.

Drop Off Your Donations at Lady L Jersey City

📍 Lady L Jersey City — West Side Avenue, Jersey City, NJ

📅 When: Year-round

🎒 What: New, unused school supplies and classroom essentials

💜 Who: Anyone who wants to support local education and the Jersey City community

Stop by during regular business hours to contribute to our ongoing collection.

Community Isn’t Just Where We Do Business. It’s Who We Show Up for.

At Lady L, we believe a stronger Jersey City starts with people looking out for one another.

Thank you to every neighbor, customer, family member, and community supporter who helps make initiatives like this possible.

Together, we can make sure the spirit of back-to-school giving lasts long after the first school bell rings.

Small acts. Big community impact. All year long.

With love,

The Lady L Team 💜
