Why We Love Wana

03/30/2026

Gummies That Go Beyond the High

At Lady L, we’re always on the lookout for brands that not only offer a quality product, but also align with our values—like care, community, and conscious cannabis. That’s why we’re so proud to carry Wana, one of the most trusted and innovative names in edibles.

Whether you’re brand new to cannabis or a seasoned enthusiast, Wana gummies offer something special: predictable, delicious, plant-powered relief. Their motto says it all: Enhance your life. And we couldn’t agree more.

Science-Backed. Plant-Based. Purpose-Driven.

Wana isn’t just here to get you high—they’re here to help you feel better. Their gummies are:

  • Consistently dosed so you know exactly what to expect
  • Vegan and gluten-free with all-natural ingredients
  • Crafted with cannabinoids that fit your lifestyle, including THC, CBD, CBN, and cutting-edge options like THCV and CBC

From sleep support to mood boosts, Wana offers formulations that make wellness feel easy, accessible, and joyful.

Why It’s a Fit for Our Community

Lady L was built for folks who want to feel at home in a dispensary—who want to explore cannabis at their own pace, without pressure or pretense. Wana gets that. Their gummies are:

  • Discreet for everyday use
  • Great-tasting with real fruit flavors
  • Designed for real life, whether that’s winding down after work, managing stress, or just enjoying a moment to yourself

And for our core customers—especially women, caregivers, professionals, and folks using cannabis for sleep or relaxation—Wana’s approachable and thoughtful vibe is right on point.

Start Low, Feel Good

Not sure where to begin? Ask our budtenders to recommend a Wana gummy that matches your needs. Whether you’re seeking deep rest, daytime focus, or a gentle mood lift, there’s a Wana product for you.

We’ll help you follow the golden rule of edibles: Start low, go slow.

Come Taste the Hype

We carry a curated selection of Wana gummies on our menu—and we’re always updating it based on your feedback. If you’ve been curious about edibles but didn’t know where to start, this is a great entry point.

Come by Lady L and see why Wana is one of the most loved edible brands in the country. We think it’s the perfect match for our customers—and for the kind of cannabis culture we’re proud to build here in Jersey City.
