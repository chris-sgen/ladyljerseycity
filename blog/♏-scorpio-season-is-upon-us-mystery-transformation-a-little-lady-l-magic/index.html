♏ Scorpio Season Is Upon Us: Mystery, Transformation & a Little Lady L Magic

10/24/2026

October 23 – November 21 | The Lady L Zodiac Edit

Some zodiac signs love the spotlight. Others prefer to keep everyone guessing.

And then there’s Scorpio.

As October settles over Jersey City, the days grow shorter, the evenings become cooler, and astrology enthusiasts welcome one of the zodiac’s most intriguing seasons.

Welcome to Scorpio season 2026.

Known for intensity, independence, loyalty, and a famously mysterious personality, Scorpio has inspired countless conversations about relationships, creativity, and personal transformation.

At Lady L Cannabis, we enjoy exploring the intersection of culture, personality, and cannabis education. This season, we’re taking a closer look at Scorpio’s fascinating mythology, famous personalities, and five cannabis products with names that fit the sign’s distinctive aesthetic.

Consider this your celestial culture guide—with a little Lady L personality.

🦂 Understanding Scorpio: The Zodiac’s Great Mystery

Dates: October 23 – November 21
Element: Water
Symbol: The Scorpion
Traditional ruling planet: Mars
Modern ruling planet: Pluto
Associated traits: Passion, loyalty, intuition, determination, and transformation.

Despite Scorpio’s fiery reputation, astrology classifies it as a water sign.

In traditional astrology, water signs are associated with emotions, intuition, and depth. Scorpio is also a fixed sign, symbolically connected with persistence and determination.

Scorpio’s association with transformation comes partly from its connection to Pluto in modern Western astrology.

Pluto represents change, renewal, and the exploration of life’s hidden dimensions.

Perhaps that’s why Scorpio has become synonymous with mystery.

Of course, astrology is a cultural and spiritual tradition rather than a scientifically established method of predicting personality.

But as a form of storytelling and self-expression?

Scorpio certainly provides plenty of inspiration.

🌟 Famous Scorpios: Independent Minds & Cultural Icons

Scorpio season includes birthdays belonging to some of entertainment’s most recognizable personalities.

Whoopi Goldberg | November 13

Actress, comedian, television personality, and outspoken cultural commentator, Whoopi Goldberg has built a career around individuality.

Goldberg has also publicly discussed cannabis and participated in cannabis-related business ventures.

Her career reflects several characteristics traditionally associated with Scorpio: independence, determination, and a willingness to challenge expectations.

Willow Smith | October 31

Born on Halloween, Willow Smith is known for her experimental musical style and evolving artistic identity.

Willow has publicly discussed her past cannabis use and her decision to stop, offering an example of personal reflection and change.

Her artistic evolution makes her an interesting figure in conversations about Scorpio’s traditional association with transformation.

Katy Perry | October 25

Known for elaborate performances, creative reinvention, and theatrical fashion, Katy Perry brings a colorful perspective to the Scorpio personality discussion.

Her work demonstrates how creativity and self-expression can continually evolve.

Leonardo DiCaprio | November 11

Actor and environmental advocate Leonardo DiCaprio is another famous Scorpio.

Known for carefully selected film roles and longstanding environmental activism, DiCaprio represents a different side of Scorpio’s cultural image: focus, persistence, and commitment to personal interests.

The common thread?

These individuals have built distinctive public identities through creativity, determination, and personal evolution.

🌿 The Lady L Scorpio Season Edit: Five Names Worth Exploring

Cannabis has its own rich vocabulary, shaped by cultivation history, genetics, regional influences, and contemporary branding.

Product names can be creative, unusual, nostalgic, or surprisingly sophisticated.

For this Scorpio Season Edit, we’re highlighting five names from Lady L’s product assortment that complement the themes of mystery, contrast, and individuality associated with Scorpio.

An important distinction: Zodiac signs do not determine how cannabis affects an individual. This selection is based on cultural themes, naming, and brand storytelling—not claims about effects, medical benefits, or suitability.

1. Cadillac Rainbow | Belushi Farms

Theme: Color, Contrast & Individuality

Few product names capture contrast quite like Cadillac Rainbow.

The combination of a classic American luxury reference with vivid rainbow imagery creates a memorable identity.

In our Scorpio-inspired editorial, Cadillac Rainbow represents the contrast between outward confidence and inner complexity.

It’s a reminder that names and visual identities can tell interesting stories before we learn anything about the products themselves.

2. Donnie Burger | Hamilton Farms

Theme: Character & Originality

Donnie Burger is a name with personality.

Unconventional and memorable, it stands out among more traditional cannabis cultivar names.

Hamilton Farms is part of New Jersey’s developing legal cannabis industry, where locally cultivated products contribute to the state’s growing cannabis culture.

For our zodiac edit, Donnie Burger represents Scorpio’s association with independence and originality.

3. Chem D Biscotti | Miss Grass

Theme: Heritage Meets Contemporary Culture

Cannabis naming conventions frequently reflect the history and evolution of cultivars.

Chem D Biscotti combines recognizable naming traditions with a contemporary identity.

Miss Grass is known for its distinctive brand presentation and emphasis on cannabis culture.

In our Scorpio Season Edit, this selection represents the intersection of tradition and reinvention.

4. Chocolate Peanut Butter Cups | Jersey Canna

Theme: Familiarity & Nostalgia

Some product names draw inspiration from familiar flavor combinations.

Chocolate and peanut butter have long been associated with classic American desserts, making the name immediately recognizable.

For this editorial, Jersey Canna’s Chocolate Peanut Butter Cups represent nostalgia and the contrast between Scorpio’s mysterious reputation and its traditionally described emotional depth.

Cannabis education reminder: Ingestible cannabis products may have delayed effects. Adults should review product-specific labeling, serving information, and warnings before making informed decisions.

5. Oreo | Pyramid

Theme: Layers & Duality

Scorpio is often described in astrology as a sign of contrasts.

Strength and sensitivity.

Privacy and emotional depth.

Determination and adaptability.

Oreo, from Pyramid, provides a playful reference to the idea of layers and contrasts.

It’s a fitting conclusion to an editorial collection inspired by one of astrology’s most multifaceted signs.

🔮 Five Things Scorpio Season Can Teach Us

Whether you’re a Scorpio or simply enjoy exploring astrology, the season offers several interesting themes for reflection.

1. Embrace change. Transformation is a recurring theme in Scorpio mythology.

2. Celebrate individuality. Personal interests and creative expression don’t need to follow trends.

3. Stay curious. Exploring different cultures, histories, and perspectives can broaden our understanding of the world.

4. Appreciate complexity. People rarely fit neatly into one personality category.

5. Make room for reflection. Autumn is a natural time to reconsider priorities and explore new ideas.

💜 Cannabis Culture, Community & Education in Jersey City

At Lady L Cannabis, we believe informed conversations about cannabis should be accessible, responsible, and grounded in education.

From learning about cultivation and product categories to exploring the history of cannabis in popular culture, there’s always something new to discover.

Our Zodiac Edit is one way to connect broader cultural conversations with the evolving legal cannabis landscape in New Jersey.

Whether you’re interested in astrology, pop culture, or the stories behind cannabis brands, we hope this seasonal feature offers something interesting to explore.

Scorpio season reminds us that there’s often more to a story than meets the eye.

And at Lady L, we’re always curious about the story behind the story.

Happy Scorpio season, Jersey City.
