Grand Opening

Saturday, September 13, 202511:00 AM - 9:00 PMLady L Cannabis Dispensary 547 West Side AvenueJersey City, NJ, 07304 United States

The wait is almost over! Lady L Cannabis is officially opening our doors at 547 West Side Ave, Jersey City, and we’re throwing a full-day celebration to mark the occasion. Come join us for food, fun, and festivities right here in the West Side neighborhood.

The Celebration

We’re kicking things off early across the street at 123Events (next to the library on Lex) with a lineup of local partners and special experiences:

11AM – 3PM – Early Bites, Bevs and Vibes

  • 🥟 Eat Em Up Empanadas: 200 munchie boxes of bite-sized empanadas (one meat + one cheese). Free while supplies last—first come, first serve!
  • 🍋 Hydrate & Elevate Mocktail Bar: THC-infused Lavender Lemonade + Uptown Arnold Palmer.
  • 🌈 Private Aura Photography: A one-of-a-kind aura photo experience with a personalized reading + guide card. Limited to 40 sessions—first come, first serve!

5 – 9PM – Evening Fun

  • 🍧 Rainbow Snow Shaved Ice Truck: Refreshing, colorful treats.
  • 🚌 Consumption Bus: A safe space to relax, consume, and connect.

From morning bites to evening vibes, this day is all about celebrating our neighborhood, our community, and the beginning of Lady L’s journey with you.
