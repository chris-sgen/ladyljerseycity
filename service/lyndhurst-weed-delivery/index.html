What is the minimum age for cannabis delivery in Lyndhurst?

New Jersey's adult-use cannabis law requires customers to be 21 or older to place a delivery order. Age is verified during account setup, and your driver confirms your ID at the door before completing the handoff. No exceptions apply.

What kinds of ID can I use for delivery orders?

Valid, government-issued photo IDs are accepted across the board. Driver's licenses, state-issued IDs, and passports all qualify, and out-of-state IDs are welcome. Have your ID uploaded to your Lady L account before placing your first order, and keep the physical one on hand for when your driver arrives.

Is a medical card required to receive cannabis delivery in Lyndhurst?

No medical card is needed. Lady L operates as an adult-use dispensary, open to any customer 21 or older. A confirmed account and a valid ID are the only things standing between you and your first order.

How much cannabis can a delivery order include?

New Jersey law caps each transaction at the equivalent of 28.35 grams, or one ounce, of usable cannabis. That total can draw from any combination of the following:

  • Dried Flower/Bud: 28.35 grams (1 ounce).
  • Concentrates/Resin/Oils: 4 grams (solid or liquid/vaporized oil).
  • Ingestible Items (Gummies/Edibles): 1,000 mg of THC (roughly ten 100mg packages).
  • Maximum Possession Limit: Up to 6 ounces of cannabis and cannabis products.
  • Mixing Products: You can mix and match, for example, by purchasing 1/2 ounce of flower and 2 grams of concentrates.
  • Daily Limits: The 1-ounce limit is generally considered a daily limit across all dispensaries.
What are the tax rates on Lyndhurst weed delivery orders?

New Jersey adult-use cannabis purchases include a 6.625% state sales tax, a 2% municipal transfer or user tax, where applicable, and a Social Equity Excise Fee (SEEF) of $2.50 per ounce. Every applicable charge is itemized at checkout before you confirm, and delivery orders are taxed the same as in-store purchases.
