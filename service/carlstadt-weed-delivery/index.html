How old do you have to be to place a cannabis delivery order?

Per New Jersey's adult-use cannabis laws, the minimum age is 21, and that applies to every order Lady L processes without exception. Age is verified during account registration, and your driver will confirm your ID upon arrival before completing the handoff.

What ID does Lady L require for cannabis delivery?

A valid government-issued photo ID is required twice, first uploaded to your Lady L account before your first order, and then presented in person when your driver arrives. Driver's licenses, state-issued IDs, and passports all qualify. Unexpired out-of-state IDs are accepted without issue.

Is a medical cannabis card needed to receive Lady L's Carlstadt weed delivery?

No medical card is required. Lady L is a fully adult-use operation, open to any customer 21 or older with a valid ID and a confirmed account. No referral, no card, and no special status needed.

What are the per-order purchase limits for cannabis delivery in Carlstadt?

New Jersey sets the per-transaction limit at the equivalent of one ounce, or 28.35 grams, of usable cannabis. Here's how that breaks down by product type:

  • Dried Flower/Bud: 28.35 grams (1 ounce).
  • Concentrates/Resin/Oils: 4 grams (solid or liquid/vaporized oil).
  • Ingestible Items (Gummies/Edibles): 1,000 mg of THC (roughly ten 100mg packages).
  • Maximum Possession Limit: Up to 6 ounces of cannabis and cannabis products.
  • Mixing Products: You can mix and match, for example, by purchasing 1/2 ounce of flower and 2 grams of concentrates.
  • Daily Limits: The 1-ounce limit is generally considered a daily limit across all dispensaries.
What will the taxes be on my Carlstadt cannabis delivery order?

Three charges apply to adult-use cannabis in New Jersey: a 6.625% state sales tax, a $2.50-per-ounce Social Equity Excise Fee (SEEF), and a 2% municipal transfer or user tax, where the local municipality has authorized it. Every charge is itemized at checkout before you confirm, so there are no surprises.

The sooner you contact us, the better. Once a driver is dispatched, making changes to an order may not be possible. Call us at 201-595-0706 or reach out through the website, and our team will do whatever we can to help.
