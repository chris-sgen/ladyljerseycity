How old do you have to be to receive a Lady L delivery?

You have to be 21 or older to place a delivery order. New Jersey adult-use cannabis laws apply to every transaction, without exception. Age is verified when you register your account, and your driver will confirm your ID at the door before completing the handoff.

What ID does Lady L accept for delivery?

You can use any valid government-issued photo ID, including a driver's license, state-issued ID, and passport. Out-of-state IDs are welcome. Your ID has to be uploaded to your Lady L account before your first order, and you'll need the physical ID on hand when your driver arrives.

Do I need a medical card for Cranford weed delivery?

No medical card is required. Lady L is an adult-use cannabis microdispensary, which means any customer who’s 21 or older can place a delivery order without one. A valid ID and a confirmed account are all that's needed to get started.

What are the purchase limits for a Cranford weed delivery order?

Per New Jersey law, you can purchase up to the equivalent of 28.35 grams (1 ounce) of usable cannabis per transaction. Here's how that breaks down by product type:

  • Dried Flower/Bud: 28.35 grams (1 ounce).
  • Concentrates/Resin/Oils: 4 grams (solid or liquid/vaporized oil).
  • Ingestible Items (Gummies/Edibles): 1,000 mg of THC (roughly ten 100mg packages).
  • Maximum Possession Limit: Up to 6 ounces of cannabis and cannabis products.
  • Mixing Products: You can mix and match, for example, by purchasing 1/2 ounce of flower and 2 grams of concentrates.
  • Daily Limits: The 1-ounce limit is generally considered a daily limit across all dispensaries.
What are the taxes on cannabis in New Jersey?

Adult-use cannabis purchases in New Jersey include a 6.625% state sales tax, a 2% municipal transfer or user tax where applicable, and a $2.50-per-ounce Social Equity Excise Fee (SEEF). These charges apply to delivery orders the same way they do to in-store purchases. All applicable taxes are itemized at checkout before you confirm your order.
