What age do you need to be to receive cannabis delivery in North Arlington?

New Jersey law sets the adult-use minimum at 21, and that applies to every Lady L delivery order without exception. Age is verified during account registration, and your driver will confirm your ID again upon arrival.

What ID is needed to complete a delivery?

A valid government-issued photo ID is required at two points in the process: uploaded to your Lady L account before your first order, and presented in person when your driver arrives. Driver's licenses, state-issued IDs, and passports all qualify. Out-of-state IDs are accepted.

Is a medical cannabis card required to place a delivery order?

No medical card is necessary. Lady L is an adult-use cannabis microdispensary, which means any customer 21 or older can place a delivery order with a valid ID and a confirmed account. Nothing else is required.

What’s the purchase limit for a North Arlington delivery order?

New Jersey law permits us to sell up to the equivalent of 28.35 grams, or one ounce, of usable cannabis per transaction. Here's how that breaks down across product types:

  • Dried Flower/Bud: 28.35 grams (1 ounce).
  • Concentrates/Resin/Oils: 4 grams (solid or liquid/vaporized oil).
  • Ingestible Items (Gummies/Edibles): 1,000 mg of THC (roughly ten 100mg packages).
  • Maximum Possession Limit: Up to 6 ounces of cannabis and cannabis products.
  • Mixing Products: You can mix and match, for example, by purchasing 1/2 ounce of flower and 2 grams of concentrates.
  • Daily Limits: The 1-ounce limit is generally considered a daily limit across all dispensaries.
What taxes apply to a North Arlington cannabis delivery order?

New Jersey adult-use cannabis carries a 6.625% state sales tax, a $2.50-per-ounce Social Equity Excise Fee (SEEF), and a 2% municipal transfer or user tax where applicable. Every tax is itemized at checkout before you confirm, so there are no surprises when your order goes through.

How does payment work for Lady L Cannabis delivery?

Full payment must be submitted before your order is confirmed and a driver dispatched. Lady L accepts ACH payment, and there’s also a debit option, but cash is not accepted for delivery. Set up your payment method through your Lady L account at registration, and reach the store at 201-595-0706 with any specific payment questions.

What if my delivery is taking longer than expected?

The weed delivery North Arlington customers have come to rely on from Lady L usually arrives in about one hour, though order volume, distance, and traffic can all affect that timing on any given run. If your order is taking longer than expected, contact our team directly. We stay on top of delays and keep things moving as efficiently as possible.

What if I need to change or cancel my order after submitting it?

Get in touch as soon as possible. Once your driver is on the road, modifications become difficult and may not be possible to accommodate, but our team will do everything we can to help. Reach us through the website or by calling 201-595-0706.

Who needs to be available when my delivery arrives?

An adult who’s 21+ with a valid government-issued ID must be present when the driver gets there. The handoff can’t be completed without someone meeting that requirement at the door. Plan to be available and reachable during your estimated delivery window.

Are there deals or discounts on North Arlington weed delivery?

Yes, though delivery deals differ from in-store promotions. Loyalty points apply to delivery orders at the same rate as they do in-store: every dollar spent earns 1 point, and 100 points equal $5 in store credit.

Also, we offer discount programs for first-time customers, veterans and military members, seniors, and several other groups. Deals rotate, so always check with our team or browse the promotions section on the website to see what's currently active.

Should I tip my delivery driver?

Tips are never required, but our drivers consistently go out of their way to make every delivery smooth, discreet, and professional. If your experience was exactly what you hoped for, a tip in the 15-20% range is a meaningful way to show your appreciation.
