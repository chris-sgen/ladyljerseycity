How old do you have to be for cannabis delivery in Wallington?

New Jersey's adult-use cannabis law sets the minimum age at 21, and that applies to every order without exception. Age is confirmed during account setup, and your driver will check your ID again at the door before the handoff.

What ID is accepted for a Lady L delivery order?

Any valid, government-issued photo ID qualifies. Driver's licenses, state-issued IDs, and passports are all accepted, and out-of-state IDs are welcome. Upload your ID to your Lady L account before your first order, and have the physical copy ready when your driver arrives.

Is a medical cannabis card required to place a delivery order in Wallington?

No medical card is needed. Lady L operates as an adult-use dispensary, open to any customer 21 or older. A confirmed Lady L account and a valid ID are the only requirements.

What’s the purchase limit for a single Wallington delivery order?

New Jersey law caps each transaction at the equivalent of 28.35 grams, or one ounce, of usable cannabis. That total can include any combination of the following:

  • Dried Flower/Bud: 28.35 grams (1 ounce).
  • Concentrates/Resin/Oils: 4 grams (solid or liquid/vaporized oil).
  • Ingestible Items (Gummies/Edibles): 1,000 mg of THC (roughly ten 100mg packages).
  • Maximum Possession Limit: Up to 6 ounces of cannabis and cannabis products.
  • Mixing Products: You can mix and match, for example, by purchasing 1/2 ounce of flower and 2 grams of concentrates.
  • Daily Limits: The 1-ounce limit is generally considered a daily limit across all dispensaries.
What taxes apply to a cannabis delivery order in Wallington?

New Jersey adult-use cannabis purchases carry a 6.625% state sales tax, a 2% municipal transfer or user tax where applicable, and a $2.50-per-ounce Social Equity Excise Fee (SEEF). These apply equally to delivery orders, and every applicable charge is itemized at checkout before you confirm.
