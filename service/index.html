Delivery Tips for the Best Experience

  • Double-check your address before placing your order.
  • Make sure your government-issued ID is current.
  • Keep your phone nearby after placing your order.
  • Answer calls or text messages from our delivery team if they need assistance locating your address
  • Plan to receive your delivery at the address provided during checkout.
  • Order early during weekends and holidays for the greatest scheduling flexibility.

Important Reminder

New Jersey law only allows cannabis deliveries to private residences and approved delivery locations. Deliveries to public places—including parks, beaches, bars, restaurants, parking lots, or other public spaces—are prohibited.
