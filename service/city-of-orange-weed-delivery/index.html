How old do I need to be to get cannabis delivered in Orange?

You must be 21+ to place a delivery order with Lady L. New Jersey's adult-use cannabis rules apply to every transaction without exception. Age is verified during account setup, and your driver will check your ID at the door before completing the handoff.

Which IDs does Lady L accept for delivery?

Any valid, government-issued photo ID will work, including driver's licenses, state-issued IDs, and passports. Out-of-state IDs are welcome. Your ID must be uploaded to your Lady L account before placing your first order, and you’ll need to present the physical ID when your driver arrives.

Do I need a medical card to get cannabis delivered?

Nope. No medical card is needed. Lady L operates as an adult-use dispensary, open to any customer 21 or older. A confirmed Lady L account and a valid ID are the only requirements to get started.

What's the purchase limit for a single cannabis delivery order?

New Jersey law allows you to buy up to the equivalent of 28.35 grams, or one ounce, of usable cannabis per transaction. That can include any combination of the following:

  • Dried Flower/Bud: 28.35 grams (1 ounce).
  • Concentrates/Resin/Oils: 4 grams (solid or liquid/vaporized oil).
  • Ingestible Items (Gummies/Edibles): 1,000 mg of THC (roughly ten 100mg packages).
  • Maximum Possession Limit: Up to 6 ounces of cannabis and cannabis products.
  • Mixing Products: You can mix and match, for example, by purchasing 1/2 ounce of flower and 2 grams of concentrates.
  • Daily Limits: The 1-ounce limit is generally considered a daily limit across all dispensaries.
What taxes will I pay on a cannabis delivery order in New Jersey?

New Jersey adult-use cannabis purchases include a 6.625% state sales tax, a 2% municipal transfer or user tax, where applicable, and a $2.50-per-ounce Social Equity Excise Fee (SEEF). All of these apply to delivery orders exactly as they do to in-store purchases, and every applicable charge is itemized at checkout before you confirm.
