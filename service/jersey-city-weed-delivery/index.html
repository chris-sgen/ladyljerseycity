Jersey City Weed Delivery FAQ

Where does Lady L Cannabis deliver in Jersey City, NJ?

Lady L Jersey City weed delivery offers services in our primary ZIP codes, including 07097, 07302, 07304, 07305, 07306, 07307, 07310, and 07311.

Beyond the city, we reach dozens of surrounding communities across Hudson, Essex, Bergen, Union, and Passaic counties. From Bayonne to Hoboken, Secaucus to Newark, and further out into the suburbs, our Zone 2 coverage covers a broad footprint.

What are Lady L's delivery hours in Jersey City?

Jersey City weed delivery runs Sunday from noon to 7:00 PM, Monday through Thursday from noon to 8:00 PM, and Friday and Saturday from noon to 9:00 PM. Those hours give you a solid window throughout the week and a later cutoff on weekends when it counts. All orders must be placed and confirmed before hours close.

How old do you have to be for weed delivery in Jersey City?

You must be 21 or older to place a delivery order. New Jersey's adult-use cannabis rules apply to every transaction we process. Age verification is part of account setup, and your driver will also verify your ID at the door before handing over your order.

What ID is accepted for cannabis delivery?

Any valid, government-issued photo ID works. That includes driver's licenses, state-issued IDs, and passports. Out-of-state IDs are accepted. Your ID needs to be uploaded to your Lady L account before you can place a delivery order, and you'll need the physical ID present when your driver arrives.

Do I need a medical cannabis card for delivery?

No medical card required. Lady L Cannabis operates as an adult-use dispensary. Any customer who is 21 or older can shop with us, and the same applies to delivery. Just a valid ID and a confirmed account are all you need.

How much cannabis can I buy in one delivery order?

We are allowed to sell up to the equivalent of 28.35 grams (1 ounce) of usable cannabis per transaction. This can be made up of any combination of the following:

  • Dried Flower/Bud: 28.35 grams (1 ounce).
  • Concentrates/Resin/Oils: 4 grams (solid or liquid/vaporized oil).
  • Ingestible Items (Gummies/Edibles): 1,000 mg of THC (roughly ten 100mg packages).
  • Maximum Possession Limit: Up to 6 ounces of cannabis and cannabis products.
  • Mixing Products: You can mix and match, such as purchasing 1/2 ounce of flower and 2 grams of concentrates.
  • Daily Limits: The 1-ounce limit is generally considered a daily limit across all dispensaries.
What taxes apply to cannabis delivery in Jersey City?

Adult-use cannabis purchases in New Jersey include a 6.625% state sales tax, a 2% municipal transfer or user tax where applicable, and a $2.50 per ounce Social Equity Excise Fee (SEEF). These apply to delivery orders the same as in-store purchases. All applicable taxes are shown at checkout before you confirm your order.

What payment methods are accepted for delivery?

Delivery orders require full payment to be submitted and captured before the order is placed and dispatched. If you have questions on how to pay for your delivery, please contact the store directly at 201-595-0706. Set up your ACH payment through your Lady L account when you create your profile. Cash is not accepted for delivery orders.

What happens if my delivery is late?

Typical delivery time is approximately one hour, though timing can vary based on order volume, traffic, and distance. If your delivery is running longer than expected, reach out to our team. We communicate proactively when delays come up and work to keep every order moving as efficiently as possible.

Can I cancel or modify my delivery order?

Contact us as quickly as possible if you need to make a change. Once an order is dispatched, modifications may not be possible, but our team will do everything we can to help. Reach us through the website or by phone at (201) 595-0706.

Do I need to be present when my delivery arrives?

Yes. An adult 21 or older must be present and available to show a valid government-issued ID upon the driver's arrival. If no one is available at the time of delivery, the order cannot be completed. Plan accordingly so you're available during your estimated delivery window.

Does Lady L offer deals on Jersey City weed delivery orders?

Yes, though delivery deals differ from in-store promotions. Loyalty points apply to delivery orders at the same rate as in-store, so every dollar spent earns one point. Every 100 points equals $5 in store credit.

First-time customers, veterans and military members, seniors, and several other groups are eligible for discount programs. Deals rotate, so always check with our team or browse the promotions section on the website for what's currently active.

How much should I tip my delivery driver?

Tipping is not required, but it's always appreciated. Our drivers are professionals who take their work seriously and go out of their way to deliver a smooth, discreet experience. A tip of 15-20% is a meaningful way to say thank you for reliable service!
