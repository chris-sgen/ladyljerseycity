How old do you have to be to get cannabis delivered in Hillside?

New Jersey's adult-use cannabis law is clear: you must be 21 or older to place a delivery order. Your age is verified when you create your Lady L account, and the driver will confirm your ID at the door before they complete the handoff. No exceptions apply.

What ID does Lady L accept for delivery?

Any valid, government-issued photo ID is accepted, including driver's licenses, state-issued IDs, and passports. Out-of-state IDs are welcome. Your ID must be uploaded to your Lady L account before your first order, and the physical ID must be present when your driver arrives.

Do I need a medical cannabis card for Hillside weed delivery?

No medical card is required. Lady L is an adult-use dispensary open to any customer 21 or older. A confirmed Lady L account and a valid ID are everything you need to place your order.

What is the purchase limit for a single cannabis delivery order?

New Jersey law allows us to sell up to the equivalent of 28.35 grams, or one ounce, of usable cannabis per transaction. That total can include any combination of the following:

  • Dried Flower/Bud: 28.35 grams (1 ounce).
  • Concentrates/Resin/Oils: 4 grams (solid or liquid/vaporized oil).
  • Ingestible Items (Gummies/Edibles): 1,000 mg of THC (roughly ten 100mg packages).
  • Maximum Possession Limit: Up to 6 ounces of cannabis and cannabis products.
  • Mixing Products: You can mix and match, for example, by purchasing 1/2 ounce of flower and 2 grams of concentrates.
  • Daily Limits: The 1-ounce limit is generally considered a daily limit across all dispensaries.
What are the taxes on cannabis delivery orders in New Jersey?

New Jersey adult-use cannabis purchases include a 6.625% state sales tax, a 2% municipal transfer or user tax, where applicable, and a $ 2.50-per-ounce Social Equity Excise Fee (SEEF). These apply equally to delivery orders, and every applicable charge is itemized at checkout before you confirm.

What are the payment options for Lady L delivery orders?

Delivery orders are processed through your Lady L account via ACH. There’s a debit option, but cash is not accepted for delivery. Orders must be paid in full before they’re confirmed and dispatched. If you have any payment questions, reach out to us directly at 201-595-0706.

Typical delivery time runs approximately one hour, though order volume, traffic, and distance can all be factors. If your order is taking longer than expected, contact our team directly. We track every order closely and address delays as quickly as possible.

Can I modify or cancel my order after confirming it?

Contact us as soon as possible if anything needs to change. Once an order has been dispatched, modifications may not be possible, but our team will do everything we can to help. Reach us by phone at 201-595-0706 or through the website.

Do I need to be home when my delivery driver arrives?

Yes. An adult 21 or older must be present and show a valid government-issued ID when the driver arrives. If no one is available at the time of delivery, the handoff can’t be completed. Please make sure you're home and available during your estimated delivery window to avoid any delays.
