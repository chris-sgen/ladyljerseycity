What's the age requirement for cannabis delivery in Carteret?

New Jersey sets the minimum age for adult-use cannabis at 21, and that applies to every delivery order Lady L processes. Age verification happens when you create your account, and your driver will ask to see your ID in person before completing the handoff. There are no exceptions.

Which forms of ID does Lady L accept for delivery?

Government-issued photo ID is what you need, and most standard forms qualify: driver's licenses, state-issued identification cards, and passports all work. Out-of-state IDs are accepted. Have your ID uploaded to your Lady L account before placing your first order, and keep the physical document on hand for when your driver arrives.

Does Lady L require a medical cannabis card for a delivery order?

Not at all. Lady L is an adult-use operation, which means the only things standing between you and a delivery order are a valid ID and a confirmed account. No medical card, no referral, no special status required.

What are the purchase limits for a single Carteret delivery?

New Jersey law caps each transaction at the equivalent of 28.35 grams, or one ounce, of usable cannabis. That limit applies across all product types combined, and here's how it breaks down:

  • Dried Flower/Bud: 28.35 grams (1 ounce).
  • Concentrates/Resin/Oils: 4 grams (solid or liquid/vaporized oil).
  • Ingestible Items (Gummies/Edibles): 1,000 mg of THC (roughly ten 100mg packages).
  • Maximum Possession Limit: Up to 6 ounces of cannabis and cannabis products.
  • Mixing Products: You can mix and match, for example, by purchasing 1/2 ounce of flower and 2 grams of concentrates.
  • Daily Limits: The 1-ounce limit is generally considered a daily limit across all dispensaries.
What taxes will I see on my Carteret delivery order?

New Jersey adult-use cannabis carries a 6.625% state sales tax, a $2.50-per-ounce Social Equity Excise Fee (SEEF), and a 2% municipal transfer or user tax where the local municipality has authorized it. All applicable charges are itemized at checkout, so there are no surprises when you confirm.

How does Lady L handle payment for delivery orders?

Payment is collected in full before your order is confirmed and a driver dispatched. Lady L accepts ACH payment and also offers a debit option for delivery customers. Cash is not accepted for delivery. Set up your payment method through your Lady L account at registration, and reach out to the store at 201-595-0706 if you have specific questions about the payment process.

What should I do if my delivery hasn't arrived yet?

Most deliveries land within about an hour, but distance, traffic, and order volume can all push that estimate in either direction on a given day. If your order is running longer than you expected, contact our team directly. We don't leave customers in the dark when delays come up.

What if I need to change or cancel my order after submitting it?

Time matters here. Get in touch as soon as possible if you need to make a change, because once your driver is on the road, modifications become difficult or impossible to accommodate. Call us at 201-595-0706 or reach out through the website, and we'll do what we can.

Do I have to be the one to answer the door when my delivery arrives?

Any adult 21 or older with a valid government-issued ID can accept the delivery. What's non-negotiable is that someone who meets that requirement is present and available when the driver arrives. If no one is there to receive the order, the driver can’t complete the handoff or leave the package unattended.

Are there deals available for Carteret weed delivery customers?

Absolutely. Our rotating delivery deals differ from in-store promotions, so it's always worth checking in with our team or browsing the promotions on our website to see what's currently running.

Meanwhile, loyalty points apply to delivery orders at the same rate as in-store purchases. That means every dollar spent on Carteret weed delivery earns one point, and every 100 points equals $5 in store credit. Lady L also offers dedicated discount programs for specific groups, including first-time customers, veterans and military members, seniors, and several others.

Should I tip the driver?

Tips are optional but always meaningful. Lady L drivers bring professionalism and discretion to every delivery, and if your experience was smooth from start to finish, a tip in the 15-20% range is a solid way to show you appreciate the effort.
