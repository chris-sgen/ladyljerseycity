How old do you need to be to get cannabis delivery in Roselle Park?

New Jersey's adult-use cannabis law is straightforward on this: you must be 21 or older to place a delivery order. Age is verified when you create your Lady L account, and your driver will confirm your ID at the door before the handoff is completed. No exceptions apply.

Which IDs does Lady L accept for delivery orders?

Any valid, government-issued photo ID is accepted. That includes driver's licenses, state-issued IDs, and passports. Out-of-state IDs are welcome. Your ID must be uploaded to your Lady L account before your first order, and the physical ID must be present when your driver arrives.

Do I need a medical card to get Roselle Park weed delivery?

No medical card is required. Lady L operates as an adult-use dispensary, open to any customer 21 or older. A confirmed Lady L account and a valid ID are all you need to place your first order.

How much cannabis can I include in a single delivery order?

New Jersey law allows up to the equivalent of 28.35 grams, or one ounce, of usable cannabis per transaction. That total can be made up of any combination of the following:

  • Dried Flower/Bud: 28.35 grams (1 ounce).
  • Concentrates/Resin/Oils: 4 grams (solid or liquid/vaporized oil).
  • Ingestible Items (Gummies/Edibles): 1,000 mg of THC (roughly ten 100mg packages).
  • Maximum Possession Limit: Up to 6 ounces of cannabis and cannabis products.
  • Mixing Products: You can mix and match, for example, by purchasing 1/2 ounce of flower and 2 grams of concentrates.
  • Daily Limits: The 1-ounce limit is generally considered a daily limit across all dispensaries.
What taxes apply to a Roselle Park cannabis delivery order?

New Jersey adult-use cannabis purchases include a 6.625% state sales tax, a 2% municipal transfer or user tax, where applicable, and a $ 2.50-per-ounce Social Equity Excise Fee (SEEF). All of these apply equally to delivery orders, and every applicable charge is itemized at checkout before you confirm.
