How old do I need to be to get cannabis delivery in Vauxhall?

New Jersey's adult-use cannabis law requires you to be 21 or older to place a delivery order. Age is verified when you set up your Lady L account, and your driver will check your ID at the door before the handoff is completed. No exceptions apply.

Which IDs does Lady L accept for delivery?

Any valid, government-issued photo ID works. Driver's licenses, state-issued IDs, and passports all qualify, and out-of-state IDs are accepted, too. Your ID must be uploaded to your Lady L account before your first order, and you’ll need to have the physical ID on hand when your driver arrives.

Do I also need a medical card to get cannabis delivered in Vauxhall?

No medical card is required. Lady L is an adult-use dispensary open to any customer 21 or older. A confirmed Lady L account and a valid ID are all you need to place your Vauxhall weed delivery order.

What's the purchase limit for a single order?

New Jersey law allows us to sell up to the equivalent of 28.35 grams, or one ounce, of usable cannabis per transaction. That can include any combination of the following:

  • Dried Flower/Bud: 28.35 grams (1 ounce).
  • Concentrates/Resin/Oils: 4 grams (solid or liquid/vaporized oil).
  • Ingestible Items (Gummies/Edibles): 1,000 mg of THC (roughly ten 100mg packages).
  • Maximum Possession Limit: Up to 6 ounces of cannabis and cannabis products.
  • Mixing Products: You can mix and match, for example, by purchasing 1/2 ounce of flower and 2 grams of concentrates.
  • Daily Limits: The 1-ounce limit is generally considered a daily limit across all dispensaries.
What taxes apply to cannabis in New Jersey?

New Jersey adult-use cannabis purchases include a 6.625% state sales tax, a 2% municipal transfer or user tax, where applicable, and a $ 2.50-per-ounce Social Equity Excise Fee (SEEF). All of these apply equally to delivery orders, and every applicable charge is itemized at checkout before you confirm.
