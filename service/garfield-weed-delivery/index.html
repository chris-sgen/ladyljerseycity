How old do you need to be to receive a Lady L delivery in Garfield?

The minimum age for adult-use cannabis in New Jersey is 21, full stop. That applies to every order Lady L processes without exception. Your age is verified when you register your account, and your driver will check your ID upon arrival and before the handoff.

What identification is needed to receive a cannabis delivery?

A valid government-issued photo ID is required at two points: uploaded to your Lady L account before your first order, and presented in person when your driver arrives. Driver's licenses, state-issued IDs, and passports all qualify. Out-of-state IDs are accepted.

Is a medical cannabis card required to place a delivery order in Garfield?

Not at all. Lady L is an adult-use cannabis microdispensary, which means any customer 21 or older can place a delivery order with a valid ID and a confirmed account. No medical card required.

What are the purchase limits for a delivery order from Lady L?

New Jersey law caps each transaction at the equivalent of 28.35 grams, or one ounce, of usable cannabis. Here's how that breaks down across product types:

  • Dried Flower/Bud: 28.35 grams (1 ounce).
  • Concentrates/Resin/Oils: 4 grams (solid or liquid/vaporized oil).
  • Ingestible Items (Gummies/Edibles): 1,000 mg of THC (roughly ten 100mg packages).
  • Maximum Possession Limit: Up to 6 ounces of cannabis and cannabis products.
  • Mixing Products: You can mix and match, for example, by purchasing 1/2 ounce of flower and 2 grams of concentrates.
  • Daily Limits: The 1-ounce limit is generally considered a daily limit across all dispensaries.
What taxes will I see on a Garfield weed delivery order?

New Jersey adult-use cannabis carries a 6.625% state sales tax, a $2.50-per-ounce Social Equity Excise Fee (SEEF), and a 2% municipal transfer or user tax, where applicable. Every charge is itemized at checkout before you confirm, so there are no surprises on the back end.

What if my delivery takes longer than expected?

Delivery typically happens within about one hour, though order volume, traffic, and distance can affect timing. If your order is running longer than expected, reach out to our team directly, and we'll keep you informed. We communicate proactively when delays come up and work to keep every order moving as efficiently as possible.

Can I make changes to my Garfield weed delivery order after placing it?

Time is a factor here, so contact us as quickly as possible if anything needs to change. Once a driver is dispatched, modifications may be difficult to accommodate. Reach us by phone at 201-595-0706 or through the website, and we’ll do our best to help.

Who needs to be present when my delivery arrives?

Any adult 21 or older with a valid government-issued ID must be present and available when the driver arrives. If no one is available at the time of delivery, the order cannot be completed. Plan accordingly so you're available during your estimated delivery window.

Are there deals available for Garfield weed delivery customers?

Yes, though delivery deals differ from in-store promotions. Loyalty points apply to delivery orders at the same rate as in-store: every dollar spent earns 1 point, and 100 points equal $5 in store credit.

In addition, first-time customers, veterans and military members, seniors, and several other groups are eligible for discount programs. Deals rotate, so always check with our team or browse the promotions section on the website for what's currently active.

Should I tip my Lady L delivery driver?
