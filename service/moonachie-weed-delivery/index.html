What’s the age minimum to get cannabis delivery in Moonachie?

New Jersey's adult-use cannabis law sets the minimum at 21, and every Lady L delivery order follows that without exception. Age is verified when you register your account, and your driver will confirm your ID upon arrival before the handoff.

What do I need for cannabis delivery?

A valid government-issued photo ID is required at two points: uploaded to your Lady L account before your first order and presented in person when your driver arrives. Driver's licenses, state-issued IDs, and passports all qualify. Out-of-state IDs are accepted.

Does Lady L require a medical cannabis card for delivery in Moonachie?

No medical card is required. Lady L is a fully adult-use operation, and any customer 21 or older with a valid ID and a confirmed account can place a delivery order. Nothing else is needed to get started.

How much cannabis can I order?

In New Jersey, the per-transaction limit is one ounce, or 28.35 grams, of usable cannabis. Here's how that breaks down across product types:

  • Dried Flower/Bud: 28.35 grams (1 ounce).
  • Concentrates/Resin/Oils: 4 grams (solid or liquid/vaporized oil).
  • Ingestible Items (Gummies/Edibles): 1,000 mg of THC (roughly ten 100mg packages).
  • Maximum Possession Limit: Up to 6 ounces of cannabis and cannabis products.
  • Mixing Products: You can mix and match, for example, by purchasing 1/2 ounce of flower and 2 grams of concentrates.
  • Daily Limits: The 1-ounce limit is generally considered a daily limit across all dispensaries.
What taxes apply to a Moonachie weed delivery order?

Adult-use cannabis purchases in New Jersey are subject to three taxes: a 6.625% state sales tax, a $2.50-per-ounce Social Equity Excise Fee (SEEF), and a 2% municipal transfer or user tax where the local municipality has authorized it. All charges are itemized at checkout before your order is confirmed.
