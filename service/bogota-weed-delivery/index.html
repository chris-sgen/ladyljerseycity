How old do you have to be to place a cannabis delivery order?

New Jersey adult-use cannabis law sets the minimum age at 21. Age is verified when you create your Lady L account, and your driver will check your ID at the door before completing the handoff. No exceptions can be made.

What forms of ID does Lady L accept for delivery?

Any valid, government-issued photo ID is accepted, including driver's licenses, state-issued IDs, and passports. Out-of-state IDs are welcome. You’ll need to upload your ID to your Lady L account before placing your first order and have the physical ID on hand when your driver arrives.

Is a medical cannabis card required to receive a delivery?

Nope, you don’t need a medical card. Lady L is an adult-use dispensary, open to any customer 21 or older. A confirmed Lady L account and a valid ID are the only requirements.

How much cannabis can I order in a single Bogota delivery?

New Jersey law allows us to sell up to the equivalent of 28.35 grams, or one ounce, of usable cannabis per transaction. That total can include any combination of the following:

  • Dried Flower/Bud: 28.35 grams (1 ounce).
  • Concentrates/Resin/Oils: 4 grams (solid or liquid/vaporized oil).
  • Ingestible Items (Gummies/Edibles): 1,000 mg of THC (roughly ten 100mg packages).
  • Maximum Possession Limit: Up to 6 ounces of cannabis and cannabis products.
  • Mixing Products: You can mix and match, for example, by purchasing 1/2 ounce of flower and 2 grams of concentrates.
  • Daily Limits: The 1-ounce limit is generally considered a daily limit across all dispensaries.
What taxes apply to cannabis delivery orders in New Jersey?

New Jersey adult-use cannabis purchases include a 6.625% state sales tax, a 2% municipal transfer or user tax, where applicable, and a $2.50-per-ounce Social Equity Excise Fee (SEEF). All of these apply equally to delivery orders, and every applicable charge is itemized at checkout before you confirm.

How is payment handled for Lady L delivery orders?

Delivery orders are processed through your Lady L account via ACH. A debit option is also available, but cash is not accepted for delivery. Full payment must be submitted before your order is confirmed and dispatched. For any payment questions, reach out directly at 201-595-0706.

What should I do if my Bogota weed delivery takes longer than expected?

Typical delivery time runs approximately one hour, though traffic, order volume, and distance can all play a role. If your order is running late, contact our team directly. We monitor every order closely and communicate delays as soon as we're aware of them.

Can I modify or cancel my order after placing it?

Reach out to us as soon as possible if anything needs to change. Once an order has been dispatched, modifications may not be possible, but our team will do everything we can to help. Contact us by phone at 201-595-0706 or through the website.

Do I need to be available when my delivery arrives?

Yes. An adult 21 or older must be present and ready to show a valid government-issued ID when the driver arrives. If no one is available at the time of delivery, the order cannot be completed. Make sure you're home and available during your estimated delivery window.

Delivery deals differ from in-store promotions, but loyalty points apply to every delivery order at the same rate as in-store purchases. That means every dollar spent on Bogota weed delivery earns one point, and every 100 points equals $5 in store credit.

Also, discount programs are available for first-time customers, veterans and military members, seniors, and several other groups. Deals rotate regularly, so check the promotions section on our website or reach out to the team to see what's currently running.

Is tipping my delivery driver expected?
