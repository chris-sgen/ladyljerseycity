Questions and answers

Faqs

How do I track points?

We track them for you. Check your receipt, ask a budtender, or peek at your account link.

Do I need a phone number?

In order to sign up for our Rewards Program powered by Alpine IQ, you’ll need to either provide a phone number or email address. We need at least one of these to create a unique member wallet to store points.

Do points expire?

Points earned through purchase expire 365 days from issue date.

Points gifted to you by staff, for your birthday or any other way besides purchase never expire as long as you have an active account!

How does the referral program work?

Refer a friend via your own unique and personal Alpine IQ referral link. This can be found in any emails you may reveive from us.

Share that link and get 100 points for every friend that signs up. They also receive 10% off their first purchase.

When do points post?

Please allow up to 24 hours for points earned via purchase to appear in your AIQ wallet.

Anything I can’t earn or redeem on?

Points aren’t earned on taxes, tips, or gift cards and can’t be used to pay taxes or fees. Some promo-priced items may be excluded—ask us!

Can I stack discounts?

You can use points with many promos, but some offers can’t be combined. We’ll always try to get you the best value.

The Fine (But Friendly) Print

  • Program rules, earning rates, and exclusions may change—current details always posted here and in-store.
